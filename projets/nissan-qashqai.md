# Nissan Qashqai

This Android tablet app presented the Nissan Qashqai as an interactive demonstration. It allowed users to explore the vehicle's features through videos and a 360° gallery. A QR code also opened an augmented reality view. The journey brought together several ways to explore the vehicle from the tablet, rather than being limited to a descriptive sheet.

At Big Boss Studio, I developed the entire Android app in Java. My work covered the tablet demonstration journey, with its videos, 360° gallery and access to the augmented reality view through a QR code.
