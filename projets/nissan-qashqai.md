# Nissan Qashqai

This Android tablet app presented the Nissan Qashqai as an interactive demonstration. It allowed users to explore the vehicle's features through videos and a 360° gallery. A QR code also opened an augmented reality view.

## My work

At Big Boss Studio, I developed the entire Android app in Java. My work covered the tablet demonstration, with its videos, 360° gallery and access to the augmented reality view through a QR code.

## Stack

`Java` `Android` `tablet` `interactive media` `augmented reality`
